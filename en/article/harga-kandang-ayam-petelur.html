Layer Cage Price: Cost Factors & How to Get a Quote

There is no single layer-cage price for every farm — each BAE house is built to order: tier count, building length, and automation level. This guide explains the cost factors so you can budget and request an accurate quote.

Why No Fixed Price?

BAE cages are modular. Type H pullet cages run 3-8 tiers, Type A layer cages 3-4 tiers — each tier adds frames, manure belts, feed lines, and drinkers. House length follows your building. Support systems (feeding, egg collection, manure treatment, environmental control) can be partial or full. So every price is calculated per project.

4 Price Factors

  • Tier count. More tiers mean more birds per square meter — and fuller automation (egg elevators, feed trolleys matched to tier count).
  • House length and capacity. Building length sets total population (birds).
  • Equipment configuration. A basic package (cage body + feeding + drinking) costs far less than a full package (plus automatic egg collection, manure cleaning, environmental control, silo). Every BAE motor ships with extra spare parts.
  • Material and location. Q235 steel, hot-dip galvanized or zinc-aluminium alloy as standard; installation cost varies by farm location.

How to Request a BAE Quote

Prepare three facts for a fast, accurate quote: (1) target population (birds), (2) land/building size (length x width), (3) preferred type (Type H for pullets, Type A for layers in hot regions). Send them via WhatsApp — the BAE team will work out the configuration and price.
