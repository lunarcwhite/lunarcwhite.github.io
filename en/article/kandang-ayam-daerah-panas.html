Chicken Cages for Hot Regions

High temperatures cut feed intake, egg output, and disease resistance. A hot-region house must dump heat as fast as birds produce it — which is what BAE Type A is designed for.

Natural Ventilation + Maximum Capacity

Type A (3-4 tiers) works as an open house: natural airflow passes through the cage rows without tall-stack obstruction. The result is even temperature at low electricity cost — fit for Indonesia's tropical climate.

Supporting Cooling Systems

  • Cooling pad. Resin-coated paper fiber with aluminium/galvanized frames cools incoming air.
  • Fans & ventilation. Forced circulation for hot spots inside closed houses.
  • Environmental control. Automatic temperature, humidity, and ammonia control matched to growth stage.
  • Stainless + food-grade ABS drinkers. Water stays clean and cool in heat — key for disease prevention.

Operating Tips

Adjust density for the dry season, clean manure belts regularly (ammonia worsens heat stress), and stock spare fan motors. Every BAE motor already ships with extra spares.
