How to Reduce Cracked Eggs in Layer Houses

Cracked eggs are lost money. The main cause is rarely thin shells — it is impact: eggs knocking into each other on manual collection routes. The fix is a gentle, fast egg path — like BAE automatic egg collection.

Common Causes of Cracked Eggs

  • Manual collection piling eggs at one point.
  • Over-steep slopes — eggs slide and collide.
  • Rough or dirty belts shaking the eggs.
  • Late collection — eggs pile up and get trampled.

How BAE Egg Collection Works

Eggs roll gently onto smooth premium nylon-plastic and PP-composite egg belts, then elevators matched to the tier count lift them with no cross-tier pileups. The flow runs continuously from cage to sorting table — minimal human touch, near-zero egg-to-egg impact. The result: a low crack rate at high efficiency, even on large farms.

Operating Tips

Run collection on schedule (never wait for pileups), keep belts clean and tensioned, and match elevator speed to the morning laying peak.
